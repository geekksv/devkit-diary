Board won't connect?
  • Use a USB cable that carries data — many charging cables do not.
  • No port in the list? Install the driver for your board's USB chip: CP210x or CH340.
  • Stuck on "connecting"? Hold the BOOT button on the board, press Flash, and let go once writing starts.
  • Close the Arduino IDE's Serial Monitor or anything else that has the port open.
